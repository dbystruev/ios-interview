Interview Helper 1.0 (10) — What to Test

The transcript now scrolls across a single line that updates as you speak (it will marquee when there isn’t enough space) and the app matches your current spoken phrase only, showing the right answer immediately. You can tap anywhere outside an input field to hide the keyboard, and the screen stays on while the app listens with the tab bar hidden during conversation.

What to verify:
1. While speaking, watch the single‑line transcript scroll (or marquee) and confirm it shows the most recent words.
2. Speak a short phrase and check that the app finds its matching answer rather than showing “No relevant answers yet.”
3. Tap outside any input field (e.g., on the Library or Listen tab) and verify the keyboard disappears.
4. Ensure the screen stays on while the app is listening and the tab bar disappears when you start talking.
5. After stopping, confirm the tab bar returns and the app is back to normal mode.

Report crashes / issues to TestFlight feedback. Thanks!
